# BearTracks — Destination unknown, but we’ll get there together.

A two-player word game you play by sending a link. No app, no account.

**Live game:** https://rljhaines.github.io/beartracks/

### How to play
1. Open the live link, enter two names, deal.
2. A link is copied to your clipboard for Player 2 — send it to them.
3. On your turn, play a word and click **Submit Word**. The link for the next turn is copied to clipboard — send it to your opponent, probably with Ctrl-v (Windows) or Cmd-v (Mac).
4. They open the link and continue. First word must cover the center star.

This version is hosted so players just click a link. There are no files to open.

### Controls
- Type word + Enter. You'll be prompted to click or double-click the starting square. Click plays across and double-click down.
- Add coordinates, 1,1 being upper lefthand corner, e.g., `8,8,0` or `8 8` for across / `8,8,1` for down, either before or after the word to be placed.
- Or type word alone then click board for across, double-click for down.
- Submit Word copies the turn link and tells you to send it to your opponent.

### Board
Original styling and bonus layout. Bonus squares are marked TW, DW, TL, DL (3x Word, 2x Word, 3x Letter, 2x Letter). Not affiliated with Scrabble®.
